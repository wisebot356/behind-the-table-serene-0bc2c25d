THE THOUGHT

Serve the whole practice, not only the therapist’s technique.

Continuing education can strengthen clinical skill without making the business more sustainable. We want to gather Hawaiʻi massage therapists, help them compare the practice they have with what they hoped to build, and learn what support would actually help.

THE PLAN

A September path from reflection to useful action.

01 Check-in
Eight questions about the practice.
02 Result
A strength, a pressure, and one small action.
03 Gathering
A small 90-minute conversation.
04 Next move
One manageable action to begin in September.

Insurance, billing, or administrative support may fit some therapists. They are options after listening, not the campaign’s assumed destination.
