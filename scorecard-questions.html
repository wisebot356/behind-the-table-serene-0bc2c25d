Review the questions.

All eight core questions, answer choices, and the conditional follow-up. This page is for reading and discussion; nothing is collected.

Who are we doing this check-in for?

The welcome asks for your name and your practice name. People without a practice name can enter their work setting or leave that box blank.

These introductory details do not change the suggested result. After the result, “Help me take the next step” opens an email-and-permission step. In this partner version, the request is a preview only and is not sent.

Q1. Which best describes your work right now?

Choose one.

  • I run a solo practice
  • I own or help run a practice with other therapists or staff
  • I work as an employee or contractor inside someone else’s business
  • I combine employed and independent work
  • I am licensed but not practicing regularly right now
  • I am preparing to start or restart my practice
  • Something else

Q2. When you first imagined this work, what did you hope it would make possible?

Choose up to 3.

  • Meaningful work that helps people or serves my community
  • A dependable livelihood
  • More freedom or control over my time
  • A pace my body could sustain
  • A close, personal practice that stays intentionally small
  • Work I could balance with family or other responsibilities
  • I was focused on learning the work and did not have a clear business picture yet
  • Something else

“Something else” opens an optional text box, limited to 180 characters.

Q3. What is already working that you want to protect?

Choose up to 2.

  • The care I provide
  • My relationship with clients
  • My schedule or flexibility
  • My income
  • My boundaries
  • My referral relationships
  • My systems and administration
  • My physical capacity and recovery
  • My sense of purpose
  • I am still trying to identify that

Q4. During a typical month, how does your workload feel?

Choose one.

  • Lighter than I want
  • About right and fairly predictable
  • Full, but manageable
  • Fuller than feels sustainable
  • Unpredictable from month to month
  • I am not actively practicing right now

Q5. What takes the most out of you right now?

Choose up to 2.

  • Staying connected to why I began
  • Finding or retaining the right clients
  • Income consistency, pricing, or financial clarity
  • Scheduling, follow-up, paperwork, payment, or claims
  • Physical capacity, boundaries, or recovery
  • Doing everything alone or not knowing which option fits
  • I am still figuring out what the main issue is

Q6. How close does your practice feel to what you hoped it would become?

Select a number from 1 to 10. 1: Very far from it. 10: It feels closely aligned. There is no ideal number. Choose the answer that feels honest today.

Q7. If the next year followed roughly the same pattern, how would you feel about that?

Choose one.

  • Comfortable; I mostly want to protect what is working
  • Open to improving a few things
  • Concerned that the current pattern will wear on me or the practice
  • I would probably need to make a significant change
  • I am too early in my practice to know

Q7B. What would you most want to protect?

Choose up to 2.

Optional follow-up, shown only when Q7 expresses concern or significant change.

  • My physical capacity
  • My enjoyment of the work
  • My income and financial stability
  • My time with family or community
  • My ability to keep serving clients well
  • My ability to keep the practice open

Q8. What kind of support would feel useful rather than overwhelming?

Choose up to 2.

  • A clear picture of what to address first
  • A small-group conversation with other Hawaiʻi therapists
  • A worksheet or self-guided resource
  • Help simplifying administration or workflows
  • Help thinking through clients, referrals, or positioning
  • A calm look at financial or practice decisions
  • A go / not-yet look at insurance-supported work
  • Ongoing peer connection
  • I am not looking for support right now
Current partner-review wording. Employees and contractors receive work-setting language in their result. Starting or returning is not treated as a failing. Timing claims and wider use await real therapist feedback.
